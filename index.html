Welcome to Srimongol

Sreemangal (often spelled Srimongol) is an upazila in the Moulvibazar District of Sylhet, famously crowned as the Tea Capital of Bangladesh. Celebrated for its sprawling landscapes of "two leaves and a bud", it features a lush blanket of over 40 rolling tea estates, tranquil wetlands, and dense tropical rainforests. It stands as one of Bangladesh's premier ecotourism destinations, drawing travelers who seek wildlife adventures, cycling paths, and indigenous cultural experiences..
